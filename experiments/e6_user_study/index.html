图像质量比较实验

谢谢参加！整个过程约 8–10 分钟。
Thank you for taking part. The study takes about 8–10 minutes.

  • 每一题有三幅画面：参考图、A、B。A 和 B 是同一个视角的两种渲染结果。
    Each trial has three pictures of the same view: the reference, A and B.
  • 点按钮在三者之间来回切换，然后选出 A 和 B 中哪一个更接近参考图。
    Switch between them with the buttons, then choose which of A and B is closer to the reference.
  • 没有时间限制；A 和 B 都至少看过一次之后才能作答。看不出差别时请凭感觉选。
    No time limit. You must look at both A and B before answering. If you see no difference, guess.
  • 最后几题是视频（循环播放），做法相同。
    The last trials are videos (looping); same task.
  • 建议用电脑；用手机请横屏，并把屏幕亮度调高。电脑键盘：R = 参考图，1 = A，2 = B。
    A computer is best; on a phone please use landscape and full brightness. Keys: R, 1, 2.
  • 不收集个人信息，只记录你的选择、用时和屏幕尺寸。
    No personal data are collected; only your choices, timing and screen size.
